Three ways to explain the same options

The product and evidence banks were shared across conditions. The response rules changed how sustainability information appeared.

C1 · Labels

Graded sustainability levels.

C2 · Supporting evidence

Adds favorable evidence for medium and high sustainability products.

C3 · Evidence and trade-offs

Addresses evidence gaps and can offer a step up at the same quality.

Slide 11 of 12

What shoppers did with the evidence

The qualitative analysis tracked how people set priorities, justified a premium and reshaped the comparison.

Define the need

Fit and expected use shaped which attributes mattered.

Justify a premium

Shoppers evaluated extra cost against the specific benefit.

Change the comparison

Shoppers requested a different product, format or level of detail.

Slide 12 of 12

What I would test next

Make the reason for a price difference visible and test whether shoppers can accurately explain it after choosing.

Show one trade-off

Compare two options and state exactly what the higher price buys.

Make gaps visible

Mark missing evidence where it could affect the choice.

Measure understanding

Test recall and interpretation alongside the selected product.
